# Colorado HOA & Condo Insurance in 2026

In Colorado it is hail — not only wildfire — pushing association premiums up the fastest. Front Range storms, a hard look at wildfire exposure after the Marshall Fire, and a brand-new state insurer of last resort have all reshaped what boards pay and what carriers will cover. Here is where the Colorado market stands in 2026 and what your board should do before renewal.

## Hail is the number-one driver of Colorado increases

Colorado reporting has been clear that hail, more than wildfire, drives the largest share of the state's homeowners and association premium increases. The Front Range is one of the costliest hail corridors in the country, and repeated roof losses have pushed carriers to raise premiums, raise roof and hail deductibles, and add cosmetic-damage exclusions. For associations with a lot of roof area, that combination is a direct hit to the budget.

## Wildfire and the wildland-urban interface

The 2021 Marshall Fire reset expectations that wildfire is an urban-edge risk in Colorado, not just a mountain one. Carriers now scrutinize wildland-urban-interface exposure closely when they underwrite association property, which affects both eligibility and price for communities near open space.

## Colorado's new insurer of last resort

Colorado stood up a FAIR Plan so property owners with no other options have a backstop. Like every FAIR Plan it is limited and basic by design — a safety net, not a bargain — so an association that ends up there should confirm a wrap policy closes the [coverage gaps](/coverage-gap-review.html) the FAIR Plan leaves open.

## Roof age, deductibles, and cosmetic exclusions

Hail-market underwriting turns on roof age and material. Watch for large percentage hail deductibles and cosmetic-damage exclusions that can leave the association holding repair costs after a storm. Reviewing your [deductible structure](/hoa-insurance-deductibles.html) line by line is one of the most useful things a Colorado board can do.

## What Colorado boards should do before renewal

Document roof age, recent replacements, and any impact-resistant upgrades — they can earn real credits. Get a current replacement-cost figure, model the hail deductible in dollars, and benchmark the [master policy](/hoa-master-insurance-policies.html) and [D&O](/hoa-board-liability-insurance.html) across carriers. Start early; hail-exposed programs are taking longer to place.
