# HOA Landscaping Contract Review: Scope, Pricing & Accountability

Landscaping is often one of an association's biggest recurring vendor expenses, and one of the most common sources of owner complaints. A clear, well-reviewed contract protects both the budget and the community's appearance.

## Define the scope

Vague scopes lead to disputes. A strong contract spells out mowing frequency, trimming, seasonal cleanups, irrigation responsibility, and what counts as extra work — so expectations are clear on both sides.

## Pricing and escalators

Multi-year landscaping contracts often include annual price escalators. Reviewing them — and benchmarking against the market — keeps the association from overpaying as the term runs on.

## Accountability and exit

Performance standards, response times, and reasonable termination rights give the board leverage if service slips. We help associations review these terms before signing.
