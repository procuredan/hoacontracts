Common questions

## HOA insurance review FAQ

### How is condo insurance different?

Condo associations split responsibility between the master policy and unit owners' HO-6 policies. Gaps between the two are a common and costly problem we review.

### What about the deductible?

Master-policy deductibles — especially percentage and water deductibles — can shift large costs to owners. We review how yours is structured.
