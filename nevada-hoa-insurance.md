# Nevada HOA & Condo Insurance in 2026

Nevada — and the Las Vegas valley in particular — is packed with master-planned and high-rise communities, and their master policies have climbed with reinsurance and rebuild costs. Nevada is not a coastal or wildfire-headline state, but the dollars are large because the insured values are large. Here is what is driving Nevada association pricing in 2026 and what your board should do before renewal.

## Why Nevada premiums are rising

Nevada association premiums are rising mostly on national pressures — the cost of reinsurance passed through to master policies, construction and rebuild inflation, and larger catastrophe loads across the industry. Because high-rise and large master-planned communities carry substantial insured values, even a modest rate increase becomes a big dollar figure at the association level.

## High-rise and master-planned communities

The Las Vegas and Henderson areas are full of high-rises and large HOAs with complex master programs spanning property, general liability, umbrella, and [D&O](/hoa-board-liability-insurance.html). More value at risk means more underwriting scrutiny — and more room for a [condo or association program](/condo-association-insurance.html) to be mispriced or mismatched to the buildings it covers.

## Replacement cost and valuation

Rebuild costs are up across the Southwest, and Nevada master policies with stale valuations are commonly underinsured. A coinsurance penalty at a total loss falls on the association, so a current [replacement-cost analysis](/hoa-replacement-cost-analysis.html) is worth doing before you shop.

## Deductibles and coverage gaps

Wind, wildfire-interface, and water-damage claims all shape Nevada underwriting. Watch the deductible structure and exclusions that can shift cost back to the association and its owners — a [coverage-gap review](/coverage-gap-review.html) is the quickest way to surface them.

## What Nevada boards should do before renewal

Get a current appraisal, review your deductibles, and benchmark the [master policy](/hoa-master-insurance-policies.html) and D&O across carriers. Start 60 to 90 days out, especially for high-rise programs, which take longer to place. Shopping the program is how Nevada boards keep a large insured value from turning into an oversized premium.
