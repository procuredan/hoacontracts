## HOA insurance review FAQ

### What does an HOA master policy cover?

Typically the association's buildings and common areas, general liability, and often umbrella, directors & officers, and crime coverage. We review how yours is structured.

### What's commonly missing?

Underinsured replacement cost, thin D&O limits, and deductible structures that quietly shift risk to the association or owners are the most common gaps we find.
