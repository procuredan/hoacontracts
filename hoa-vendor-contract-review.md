# HOA Vendor Contract Review: Protecting the Association in Every Agreement

Every vendor agreement an association signs carries risk as well as cost. A consistent review process protects the community's budget and shields the board from exposure buried in the fine print.

## Insurance and indemnification

Vendors working on association property should carry adequate insurance and name the HOA as additional insured. Indemnification language decides who's responsible when something goes wrong — it's worth getting right.

## Scope, pricing, and term

Clear scope, transparent pricing, and a sensible term protect the association from disputes and runaway costs. Multi-year terms and escalators especially deserve a second look.

## A consistent review process

Reviewing vendor contracts the same way every time — insurance, scope, pricing, termination — gives boards leverage and consistency. We help associations build that review into renewals.
