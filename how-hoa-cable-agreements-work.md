# How HOA Cable & Bulk TV Agreements Work

Bulk cable and TV agreements can be a real amenity for a community — or a long-term cost that outlives its value. Understanding how they work helps your board decide whether yours still makes sense.

## The bulk model

The association contracts TV service for every unit at a discounted group rate, usually billed through dues. The trade-off is that every owner pays, whether they use the service or not.

## How they're priced

Pricing is driven by channel lineup, term length, take rate, and escalators. As streaming changes how owners watch, the value of a bulk TV agreement is worth re-evaluating at each renewal.

## What to review before renewing

Term length, auto-renewal, escalators, and equipment obligations are the key terms. A review before renewal keeps the association from locking into outdated service.
