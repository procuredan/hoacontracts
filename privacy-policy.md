# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: June 17, 2026*

## Information We Collect

HOA Contracts collects the information you submit through our forms — such as your name, email, phone number, business details, and any details about your request — so a specialist can follow up about HOA insurance.

## How We Use It

We use your information solely to respond to your request and connect you with the appropriate specialist or partner. Your information is never sold or shared with unrelated third parties.

## How You're Contacted

By submitting a form, you consent to be contacted by phone, text, and email. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to texts or asking to be removed.

## SMS & Mobile Messaging

Text messages are sent only by HOA Contracts (Procure Media, LLC), and your mobile number and SMS opt-in are never sold or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

## Data Retention & Security

We retain submissions only as long as needed to serve your request and protect them with reasonable safeguards.
