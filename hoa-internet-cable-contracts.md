# HOA Internet & Cable Contracts: Bulk Agreements, Terms & Renewals

Bulk internet and cable agreements are some of the largest long-term contracts an association signs — and some of the least reviewed. The terms your board agrees to can lock the community in for years.

## How bulk agreements work

In a bulk agreement, the association contracts service for the whole community at a negotiated per-unit rate, usually folded into dues. Done well, it lowers costs; done poorly, it locks in rates and terms that don't age well.

## What to watch in the terms

Auto-renewal clauses, annual escalators, exclusivity, and equipment obligations all deserve scrutiny. These are where multi-year contracts quietly become expensive.

## Reviewing before you sign

Because these agreements run for years, a review before signing or renewing is worth far more than the time it takes. We help boards compare terms and negotiate from a stronger position.
