# Why HOA Insurance Costs Are Rising — And What Boards Can Do

Association insurance premiums have climbed sharply in recent years. Understanding what's driving the increases helps your board respond with strategy instead of just absorbing another hike.

## What's driving the increases

Rising construction and replacement costs, more frequent severe-weather claims, and carriers pulling back from certain regions have all pushed HOA premiums up — even for communities with no claims of their own.

## What boards can control

Boards can't control the market, but they can control how their risk is presented: accurate valuations, documented maintenance, sensible deductible choices, and a clean submission to the right carriers all influence pricing.

## The cost of doing nothing

Auto-renewing without a review is how communities end up overpaying. A competitive benchmark, done before renewal, is the most reliable way to keep increases in check.
